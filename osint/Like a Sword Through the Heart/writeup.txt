The title was meant to reference the fact that he got a spear through his chest, like a sword through the heart, but not exactly. 

Comradery and brotherhood, fighting side by side -> He fought alongside Alexander the Great for many many years and famously saved his life on the field as well. 

All put to the end over a clouded fight -> In this situation, clouded was figurative referring to their final encounter being a drunken argument. 

Two men fought, one left distraught. -> As the argument intensified, Alexander grabbed a spear and impaled his friend... his friend didn't make it.  Alexander was deeply remorseful following the incident hence the one left distraught
