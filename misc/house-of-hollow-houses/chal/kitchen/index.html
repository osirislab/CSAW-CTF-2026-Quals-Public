There is a balcony on the floor. There is, properly speaking, a balcony for every floor. The house has confirmed this; the house keeps records; the records are kept in the cloister, which is, in turn, on a floor of its own.

Above the lintel of the parlour is carved a word. The word is not in any of the languages the house has been taught. The scullery has offered, on several occasions, to translate it. The offer has not, to date, been accepted.
