Ask the observatory for the time. The observatory will tell you it is later than you think. It will not specify than what.

Above the lintel of the billiard is carved a word. The word is not in any of the languages the house has been taught. The barn has offered, on several occasions, to translate it. The offer has not, to date, been accepted.

Do not enter the smithy without first consulting the transept. The transept will not answer, but it will, after a fashion, listen.

If you find yourself in the antechamber, the dining is two doors to your left, and three doors to your right, and either way you are, by the time you arrive, in the vault.

Two staircases descend from the hollow. One arrives at the apse. The other arrives at the apse. The arrival is, in either case, the same; the descent is not.

If you find yourself in the parlor, the library is two doors to your left, and three doors to your right, and either way you are, by the time you arrive, in the baptistery.

a note from the caretaker

If you stand in the dormitory and whisper the name of the apse, the apse will, very faintly, whisper back. It will not, on any occasion, whisper your name.

The lock on the door of the atelier is on the inside. The key is in the cache. Visitors are advised to plan accordingly.

A draft moves between the refectory and the balcony. The draft, on cold nights, carries the sound of a conversation that took place in the hollow, in a year the house has been asked not to specify.

Above the lintel of the lazaret is carved a word. The word is not in any of the languages the house has been taught. The mews has offered, on several occasions, to translate it. The offer has not, to date, been accepted.
