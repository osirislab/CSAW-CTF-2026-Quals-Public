a partial account

What is kept in the landing is not, in any meaningful sense, kept. It is merely, on most days, present. The visitors have been asked not to inquire further.

The lazaret keeps a list. The list is of every visitor who has ever asked, on entering, where the tower is. The list is bound; the list is long; the list is the lazaret, written down.

Of all the rooms in the house, the narthex is the one the house is least proud of. The house has been asked, on more than one occasion, to demolish it. The house has, on more than one occasion, declined.

If you stand in the attic and whisper the name of the observatory, the observatory will, very faintly, whisper back. It will not, on any occasion, whisper your name.

The gallery was decorated, in 1903, by a woman who is now in the tower. She has not, on any subsequent occasion, asked to be moved.

The larder was, at one point, twice its present size. The other half is in the presbytery, and is not, the house insists, available for inspection.
