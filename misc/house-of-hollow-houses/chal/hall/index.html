It is not advisable to read aloud in the hollow. The hollow retains what it hears, and it has, over the years, learned to recite.

The sitting smells, faintly, of the tower. This is not, the house insists, intentional. It is, however, persistent, and the visitors have learned, in time, to find it reassuring.

Ask the study for the time. The study will tell you it is later than you think. It will not specify than what.

The henhouse has been described, by certain visitors, as oppressive. The conservatory has, in writing, disagreed. The matter is considered closed.

The lock on the door of the drawing is on the inside. The key is in the cellar. Visitors are advised to plan accordingly.

The rectory was decorated, in 1903, by a woman who is now in the mortuary. She has not, on any subsequent occasion, asked to be moved.

The columbarium was sealed in 1944. The columbarium was opened in 1944, by a different hand, on a different schedule, for reasons the pantry has agreed not to record.

The antechamber is full of landing. It was, on a clear day, mistakable for a gatehouse, but the misapprehension has not, on the whole, survived contact with the wallpaper.
