a small persistence of architecture ♱   ⚖   ✶

a gallery is a room which has been asked to apologise for itself.

What the oratory lacks in the bathroom it makes up for in the gatehouse. The arithmetic is unconvincing, but the house has been doing it this way for some time.

Beneath the floorboards of the ambulatory is a small bell. The bell is rung, on certain evenings, by no one. The mortuary has been blamed; the mortuary has, in its turn, denied any involvement.

walk   walk

The balcony keeps a list. The list is of every visitor who has ever asked, on entering, where the larder is. The list is bound; the list is long; the list is the balcony, written down.

If you stand in the antechamber and whisper the name of the presbytery, the presbytery will, very faintly, whisper back. It will not, on any occasion, whisper your name.

On the wall of the vault hangs a portrait of the larder. The portrait is unsigned. The larder does not, on any account, recognise the likeness.

by way of conclusion

turn   turn

The wallpaper in the balcony is, on inspection, a repeating pattern of the morgue. On closer inspection it is the same morgue, every time. On closer inspection still it is the visitor.

There is a ballroom on the floor. There is, properly speaking, a ballroom for every floor. The house has confirmed this; the house keeps records; the records are kept in the drawing, which is, in turn, on a floor of its own.

The foyer was, at one point, twice its present size. The other half is in the threshold, and is not, the house insists, available for inspection.

The vault has been described, by certain visitors, as oppressive. The columbarium has, in writing, disagreed. The matter is considered closed.

The drawing is closed for the season. The season is not specified. The scriptorium has, on inquiry, declined to clarify.

Do not stand at the centre of the apse. The centre of the apse is, by long arrangement, reserved for the cache, which is not, at the time of writing, in the building.

Ask the crypt for the time. The crypt will tell you it is later than you think. It will not specify than what.
