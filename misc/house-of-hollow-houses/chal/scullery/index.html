a second aside

The ballroom was, in an earlier century, the parlour. The parlour was, before that, the music. The house considers these distinctions, on its better days, ornamental.

It is not advisable to read aloud in the barn. The barn retains what it hears, and it has, over the years, learned to recite.

Ask the guardroom for the time. The guardroom will tell you it is later than you think. It will not specify than what.

The cloister keeps a list. The list is of every visitor who has ever asked, on entering, where the gallery is. The list is bound; the list is long; the list is the cloister, written down.

Beneath the floorboards of the vault is a small bell. The bell is rung, on certain evenings, by no one. The reliquary has been blamed; the reliquary has, in its turn, denied any involvement.
